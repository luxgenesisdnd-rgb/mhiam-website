A New Chapter: Teaching Minds, Touching Hearts

Thursday, July 24, 2025

After more than two decades of teaching in the Philippines, the snowy remoteness of Alaska, and now the vibrant communities of Texas, I’ve come to realize one truth: teaching is more than a profession it’s a calling.

This blog is born out of that calling. It is a space to reflect, inspire, and share my journey as an educator, especially in the fields of special education, early childhood education, and supporting neurodiverse learners. From lesson strategies to faith-filled reflections, my goal is simple to teach with both wisdom and heart.

Every child I’ve taught, every culture I’ve worked in, and every challenge I’ve overcome has shaped the teacher I am today. Whether you’re just starting your teaching journey, navigating a new educational system, or advocating for students with learning differences, I hope you find something here that encourages you to keep going.

Thank you for walking with me on this journey. Let’s continue teaching minds and touching hearts together.
