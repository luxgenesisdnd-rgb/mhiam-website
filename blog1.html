Teaching Across Borders: My Journey from the Philippines to Alaska to Texas

Thursday, July 24, 2025

Teaching is more than just a job for me it’s a lifelong passion and a calling that has taken me across continents and cultures.

I started my career in the Philippines, spending nearly 19 years in classrooms filled with bright, eager young learners. Those early years taught me the heart of education: patience, dedication, and the power of connection. The vibrant culture and close-knit communities shaped not only my teaching style but also my belief in the importance of culturally responsive education.

Then came an unexpected adventure to the remote, snowy wilderness of Iditarod, Alaska. Teaching in such a unique environment was both challenging and rewarding. I learned to navigate a different school system, adapt to diverse cultural backgrounds, and embrace creativity to meet my students’ needs. The resilience I developed there, both personally and professionally, strengthened my commitment to supporting every learner, especially those with special education needs.

Today, I am fortunate to continue this journey in Texas, teaching kindergarten to a wonderfully diverse group of students. The warmth of Texas and its rich multicultural landscape inspire me to blend all my past experiences into creating inclusive, compassionate, and effective classrooms. Every day is a new opportunity to make a difference—one child, one lesson, one heart at a time.

This blog is my space to share the lessons I’ve learned, the challenges I’ve faced, and the joys of teaching across borders. Whether you’re an educator, parent, or lifelong learner, I hope my stories encourage you to embrace diversity, nurture curiosity, and teach with heart.

Thank you for joining me on this incredible journey.
