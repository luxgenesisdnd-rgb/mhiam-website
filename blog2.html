Lessons Across Lands

Thursday, July 24, 2025

My teaching journey has taken me across borders and into places of both challenge and profound growth. One of the most transformative chapters of my career was serving as a teacher in the remote area of Iditarod, Alaska. Immersed in a community shaped by rich traditions and diverse cultures, I gained invaluable experiences navigating a school system vastly different from what I had known.

Teaching is such an isolated setting required not only adaptability and strength, but also deep cultural sensitivity and compassion. It was there that I truly learned the importance of meeting students where they are honoring their background, respecting their heritage and embracing the uniqueness of each learner. The experience sharpened my teaching skills, strengthened my resilience, and deepened my commitment to educational equity for all children, regardless of geography or circumstances.
